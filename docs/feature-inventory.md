# Mac Utility Kit feature inventory

Tool catalog organized by category. See the [tool specifications](plan/README.md) for controls, outputs, and acceptance checks.

## Video (16)

- **Download Video** — Save web videos or audio from supported sites, with a choice of audio language where available.
- **Burn In Subtitles** — Render captions permanently into video using SRT or automatic transcription.
- **Extract Subtitles** — Export an embedded text subtitle track as SRT or WebVTT.
- **Change Speed** — Adjust playback speed while preserving voice pitch.
- **Extract Frames** — Export video stills as PNG, JPEG, or HEIC.
- **Record Screen** — Record a display, window, or region with audio and optional click indicators.
- **Compress Video** — Reduce video file size.
- **Convert Video** — Transcode to MP4, MOV, WebM, or M4V.
- **Resize Video** — Set the output dimensions of a clip.
- **Crop & Rotate Video** — Crop the frame and rotate or mirror footage.
- **Media Info** — Inspect codecs, bitrate, frame rate, resolution, and other media metadata.
- **Trim Video** — Cut a clip to the desired start and end without re-encoding.
- **Merge Videos** — Join clips in a chosen order.
- **Mute Video** — Strip the audio track without re-encoding.
- **Replace Audio** — Replace a soundtrack or mix narration or music into it.
- **Video to GIF** — Export a clip as an animated GIF.

## Audio (6)

- **Clean Up Audio** — Reduce noise and hum in recorded audio or video.
- **Record Audio** — Capture microphone audio and optionally transcribe it locally.
- **Trim Audio** — Cut a recording to a selected segment.
- **Merge Audio** — Reorder and join audio files with optional gaps or crossfades.
- **Extract Audio** — Export a video soundtrack as a separate file.
- **Convert Audio** — Convert among MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, and AIFF.

## Images (13)

- **Screenshot** — Capture a region, full display, window, or scrolling content. Copy it or export PNG/JPEG.
- **Blur Faces** — Detect faces and apply blur, pixelation, or solid masks.
- **Beautify Screenshot** — Style screenshots with backgrounds, margins, rounded corners, and shadows.
- **Remove Background** — Isolate photo subjects with transparent or solid-color backgrounds.
- **Convert Images** — Export images as JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, or WebP.
- **Compress Images** — Reduce image file size.
- **Resize Images** — Rescale by percentage, longest edge, or exact dimensions.
- **Rotate Images** — Rotate or mirror batches of images. Mac Utility Kit also supports cropping.
- **Remove Metadata** — Strip embedded camera, date, and location information.
- **Color Picker** — Sample screen colors and copy values such as HEX, RGB, and HSL.
- **Redact Image** — Conceal selected regions using blur, pixelation, or solid masks.
- **Annotate Screenshot** — Add arrows, boxes, labels, and numbered callouts.
- **Watermark** — Apply a text or logo overlay to photos, PDFs, and videos.

## Docs (23)

- **Scan Document** — Use an iPhone or existing photos to deskew and assemble scans into a PDF.
- **Redact PDF** — Permanently remove sensitive PDF content from selected areas.
- **Fill & Flatten Forms** — Fill fields, place text, detect form fields on flat or scanned pages, edit detected fields, and export either fillable or flattened copies.
- **Make PDF Searchable** — Add searchable and selectable OCR text to scanned PDFs.
- **PDF to Word** — Export editable Word documents with recognizable headings, lists, and paragraphs.
- **Sign PDF** — Type or draw a signature and position it on the page.
- **Merge PDFs** — Combine PDFs and images into one document.
- **Split PDF** — Create separate files from individual pages or ranges.
- **Organize PDF** — Reorder, rotate, or remove pages. Mac Utility Kit also supports page numbering.
- **Delete Pages** — Delete selected pages from a document.
- **Extract Pages** — Copy selected pages into a separate PDF.
- **Images to PDF** — Combine photos or scans into a PDF.
- **Crop PDF** — Set smaller visible page bounds.
- **Compress PDF** — Reduce PDF size by recompressing embedded images.
- **Add Password** — Encrypt PDF copies and set copying or printing restrictions.
- **Remove Password** — Save a decrypted copy of a PDF using its password.
- **PDF to Images** — Render pages to PNG, JPEG, or HEIC.
- **Convert Documents** — Convert among Word, OpenDocument, RTF, HTML, plain text, and Markdown.
- **Word to PDF** — Render Word, RTF, and text documents to PDF.
- **PowerPoint to PDF** — Render slide decks to PDF through Keynote or LibreOffice.
- **Excel to PDF** — Render spreadsheets to PDF through Numbers or LibreOffice.
- **Web Page to PDF** — Export a webpage as paginated or continuous PDF.
- **Markdown to PDF** — Render formatted Markdown to PDF.

## Text & Speech (10)

- **Transcribe** — Generate timestamped transcripts from audio or video.
- **Summarize** — Summarize text and documents with on-device Apple Intelligence.
- **Extract Text** — Run OCR on images, screenshots, or scanned PDFs.
- **Capture Text** — Copy recognized text from a selected screen region.
- **Translate** — Translate text, documents, and subtitles locally while retaining formatting and timings.
- **Text to Speech** — Read text, documents, and PDFs aloud and export audio.
- **Ask a Document** — Ask questions about a document locally and get answers with page references.
- **Word Counter** — Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs, and estimate reading time and word frequency.
- **Batch Find & Replace** — Preview literal or regular expression edits across text files and export copies.
- **Case Converter** — Convert text case and identifier styles including camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case.

## Files (5)

- **QR Code** — Create text, URL, or Wi-Fi QR codes and read QR codes or barcodes from images.
- **Batch Rename** — Apply numbering, date, case, and find-and-replace rules to filenames in bulk.
- **Find Duplicates** — Find identical files across folders and move selected duplicates to Trash.
- **Zip & Unzip** — Extract ZIP, RAR, 7z, and TAR and create ZIP archives with optional password protection.
- **Organize Downloads** — Group downloaded images by originating website in folders or Photos albums.

## Mac (18)

- **Network Speed Test** — Measure download, upload, and responsiveness using Apple's network test.
- **Clipboard History** — Search and reuse previous clipboard entries, with password-manager exclusions.
- **Storage Explorer** — Inspect disk usage and review large or expendable items for removal.
- **Wi-Fi Password & QR** — Display a saved Wi-Fi password and create a guest join code.
- **Disk Speed Test** — Measure read and write performance on internal or external drives.
- **Battery Health** — Inspect battery capacity, charge cycles, charging, and accessory levels.
- **Keep Awake** — Prevent system sleep for a chosen interval.
- **Uninstall App** — Remove an app and its associated caches, preferences, and data.
- **Presentation Mode** — Hide desktop icons and the Dock and keep the Mac awake during a presentation.
- **Clean Keyboard** — Temporarily suppress typing and optionally darken the screen for cleaning.
- **Dead Pixel Test** — Show solid colors to inspect panels for pixel and backlight defects.
- **Eject All Disks** — Eject external drives, cards, and mounted disk images together.
- **Login & Background Items** — Review login and background items, their publishers, and disable selected items.
- **Fix “App Is Damaged”** — Inspect an app's publisher and manage its download quarantine flag.
- **Reset Permissions** — Reset an app's privacy permission so macOS can prompt again.
- **Hidden Settings** — Toggle Finder, Dock, and other macOS preferences.
- **Display Control** — Adjust external monitor brightness, contrast, and volume.
- **Mouse Fixes** — Set independent mouse and trackpad scroll directions and tune wheel scrolling.

## Developer (14)

- **JSON Formatter** — Validate, pretty-print, and minify JSON with error locations.
- **Base64** — Encode or decode text and files as Base64, hex, or binary, with URL-safe and line-wrap options.
- **Hash Generator** — Compute and verify MD5, SHA-1, and SHA-2 digests for text or files.
- **UUID Generator** — Generate v4 and v7 identifiers in bulk and inspect their version and timestamp.
- **JWT Decoder** — Inspect token headers, claims, and expiration, and verify HMAC signatures.
- **Timestamp Converter** — Convert between dates and Unix time in seconds, milliseconds, or microseconds across time zones.
- **Diff Checker** — Compare text or files inline or side by side with word-level highlights.
- **Regex Tester** — Preview regex matches, capture groups, and replacements interactively.
- **Clean Links** — Strip link tracking parameters and unwrap supported redirects.
- **Coordinate Picker** — Use Apple Maps to find coordinates, measure distances, and look up lists of locations.
- **Unicode Inspector** — Inspect characters, invisible whitespace, and visually similar code points.
- **Lorem Ipsum** — Generate placeholder words, sentences, or paragraphs as text, HTML, or Markdown.
- **Password Generator** — Generate passwords or passphrases and display a strength estimate.
- **CSV ↔ JSON** — Convert CSV to JSON or Markdown tables and JSON to CSV.
