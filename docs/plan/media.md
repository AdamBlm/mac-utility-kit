# Video, audio, and image tool designs

Show the most common choices first and reveal format-specific controls under Advanced. Offer a preview where an edit changes timing, framing, appearance, or sound. File batches use one settings set with a per-file result. Show format and codec combinations supported by the detected engine and explain requirements before a job starts.

## Video

| Tool | Subfeatures and controls | Outputs and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Download Video | Paste a URL, inspect available resolutions and audio languages, choose video or audio-only, select a supported format, and review download progress with cancel and retry. | Save each completed download separately. Report unavailable streams, unsupported sources, and connection interruptions and discard incomplete output on cancel. |
| Burn In Subtitles | Import SRT or generate local captions where available. Review and edit cue text and timing, apply a global offset, set font size, color, background, and position, and preview at the current playhead. | Export video with captions visible in the rendered frames. Flag malformed or overlapping cues and verify offset and readability near clip edges. |
| Change Speed | Select a speed preset or enter a multiplier, toggle voice pitch preservation, and compare original and adjusted duration in the preview. | Export with the selected speed and synchronized audio. Block zero or unsupported multipliers and verify fast and slow clips with and without audio. |
| Extract Frames | Scrub or enter a timestamp, add multiple timestamps or a fixed interval, and choose PNG, JPEG, or HEIC and a quality setting where supported. | Save ordered frame files named by time or sequence and report skipped frames. Validate timestamps against duration and cap estimates for dense batch extraction. |
| Record Screen | Choose display, window, or adjustable region. Set frame rate and resolution, toggle system sound, microphone, and click indicators, and provide countdown, pause, and stop controls with visible recording status. | Save a playable recording with the chosen source and audio. Handle permission denial, a closed window, and disconnected audio or displays with explicit status. |
| Compress Video | Choose the output container (MP4, MOV, M4V, or WebM when supported), codec, and quality preset (Smaller File, Balanced, or Higher Quality). Advanced settings include quality or target bitrate, resolution cap, and audio bitrate. Preview a short sample, show estimated size, and apply settings to a batch. | Report original and final size and savings per file. Validate container and codec together before running. If an export is larger, flag it and let the user decide whether to keep it. Verify the selected container and that quality settings change the encoded output. |
| Convert Video | Choose MP4, MOV, M4V, or WebM, select a compatible codec, choose passthrough or transcode where supported, and optionally adjust size, frame rate, and audio settings. | Report actual media formats and any dropped tracks. Block unsupported codec and container combinations before export and verify playback. |
| Resize Video | Choose exact dimensions, percentage, or a resolution preset. Lock aspect ratio or choose fit with padding, fill with cropping, or stretch. Preview final framing. | Display resulting dimensions before export. Validate encoder alignment, handle source orientation, and confirm final dimensions in Media Info. |
| Crop & Rotate Video | Drag crop handles or enter coordinates, select freeform or aspect ratio presets, and rotate or mirror through preview controls. | Export with the chosen crop and orientation. Reject empty crops and verify pixels and rotation metadata agree with the preview. |
| Media Info | Show container, duration, dimensions, size, codec, frame rate, and bitrate. Expand video, audio, and subtitle streams and copy selected values. | Copy a text summary or export structured metadata. Label missing or estimated values and show variable frame rate separately from a nominal value. |
| Trim Video | Set in and out points by scrubber or timecode and preview the selected segment. Show keyframe-aligned lossless cuts and offer a separately labeled precise re-encode mode. | Export the selected duration with correct audio sync. Show adjusted cut times before lossless export and block inverted or zero-length ranges. |
| Merge Videos | Add and reorder clips, preview boundaries, and choose a common output format, resolution, and audio policy for mixed sources. Offer passthrough when streams are compatible. | Export in displayed order and show warnings for clips requiring normalization. Verify duration and transitions on sources with mixed dimensions or missing audio. |
| Mute Video | Preview audio tracks and choose all tracks or specific tracks to remove. Preserve video stream by default and show the remaining stream list before writing. | Verify removed tracks are absent and video is unchanged where stream copy is supported. Flag already silent inputs and incompatible containers. |
| Replace Audio | Import replacement audio or mix it with the original. Select tracks, set timing offset and volume, and choose how to handle shorter or longer audio. Preview the mix and clipping indicators. | Export with the expected track mix and duration. Verify timing for mismatched durations and block a mix selection when no source track exists. |
| Video to GIF | Mark a clip range and choose width, frame rate, looping, and palette quality. Preview animation and approximate size. | Export a GIF with the selected timing and loop behavior. Warn before very large exports and verify frame order and animation playback. |

## Audio

| Tool | Subfeatures and controls | Outputs and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean Up Audio | Import an audio file or video track, adjust noise reduction and hum removal, and A/B preview a selected segment at matched loudness. | Export cleaned audio or video with original visuals. Preserve timing and report unsupported tracks, clipping, or inputs with no sound. |
| Record Audio | Select microphone, view a level meter, pause and resume, and optionally send the completed recording for local transcription. | Save a playable recording and separate transcript when selected. Handle denied permissions, disconnected input, and interrupted recording with recoverable results where possible. |
| Trim Audio | View a waveform, set start and end by dragging or entering times, loop the selection, and optionally apply head and tail fades. Choose output format and a compatible lossless copy mode. | Verify selection boundaries and duration. Flag when sample-accurate cuts or fades require encoding and prevent empty selections. |
| Extract Audio | Choose one video audio track by language or index, preview it, and pick source passthrough or a compatible output format and quality. | Save the requested track with correct duration, language label, and audible output. Flag videos without audio and unsupported passthrough combinations. |
| Convert Audio | Choose MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, or AIFF. Adjust bitrate or quality where relevant, sample rate, and mono or stereo, with a metadata preservation option. | Return separate files and conversion details per input. Validate requested settings with the selected codec and verify playback and channel count. |

## Images

| Tool | Subfeatures and controls | Outputs and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screenshot | Select display, window, region, or scrolling capture. Offer a countdown, cursor inclusion where supported, and a review before copying, exporting PNG or JPEG, or opening in Annotate Screenshot. | Verify capture dimensions and Retina scaling. Handle unavailable sources and permission denial. Review scrolling seams and let users stop an incomplete capture. |
| Blur Faces | Show suggested face regions, allow adding and adjusting masks, and choose blur, pixelation, or solid fill with strength controls and before-and-after preview. | Export a flattened image. Highlight detection misses during review and verify opaque masks fully cover the selected area. |
| Beautify Screenshot | Adjust background color or gradient, padding, corner radius, shadow, and canvas aspect ratio. Preview changes at intended output resolution. | Export PNG or JPEG with the selected composition. PNG can preserve transparent padding when selected. Keep content visible when aspect ratios change. |
| Remove Background | Isolate the primary subject, refine the keep/remove mask, and preview over transparency or a chosen solid color. | Export transparency-capable output when selected and preview the flattened background for other formats. Check edges and handle images with no clear subject. |
| Convert Images | Choose JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, or WebP, plus quality, alpha handling, and metadata retention where applicable. Show actual output dimensions and results in a batch. | Preserve orientation and color appearance. Handle unsupported format options before export and allow a background color when flattening transparency. |
| Compress Images | Choose lossless or adjustable lossy compression, format where supported, and optional size goal. Show an A/B zoom view and size comparison. | Report per-file savings and distinguish best-effort size targets from achieved output. Preserve requested alpha and orientation and flag any file that grew. |
| Resize Images | Choose percentage, longest edge, or exact width and height. Offer aspect ratio lock, fit or fill when sizing to bounds, and an allow-upscaling option. Choose output format and preview dimensions per input. | Match dimensions and mode shown in preview. Verify portrait and landscape images, orientation tags, transparent output, and invalid size handling. |
| Rotate Images | Rotate by quarter-turns, flip horizontally or vertically, and apply a common transform to selected images. Preview before export. | Verify resulting dimensions and physical orientation in another viewer and prevent orientation metadata from rotating the output a second time. |
| Remove Metadata | Inspect metadata by group, select location or other fields to remove, and compare the before-and-after lists. Keep display-critical orientation and color information where possible. | Reopen the exported copy and verify selected fields are absent and appearance remains correct. Explain formats with limited metadata controls. |
| Color Picker | Sample screen points with a magnified picker and show HEX, RGB, and HSL. Select display precision, copy a format, and revisit recent swatches during the session. | Verify conversion against known color values and label the color space. Provide a keyboard cancel action and handle capture permission failure. |
| Redact Image | Create adjustable regions, choose solid cover, blur, or pixelation, and preview at full resolution. Provide undo and redo while editing. | Export a flattened image and present a metadata removal option. Make solid cover available for size-independent concealment and verify it at export scale. |
| Annotate Screenshot | Add arrows, lines, boxes, text, and numbered callouts. Set color and stroke, reorder or move items, and undo or redo edits. | Copy or export a flattened PNG or JPEG. Verify text legibility, ordering, and placement at full resolution. |
| Watermark | Add text or a logo, choose anchor or tiled position, adjust scale, opacity, margin, and rotation, and preview on an image, PDF page, or video frame. Select a PDF page range or video timing window where applicable. | Export a separate file and verify placement across different dimensions, page rotations, and video times without obscuring the preview controls. |
